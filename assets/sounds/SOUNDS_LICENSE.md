# Built-in sounds

The built-in packs are built by `tools/gen-builtin-sounds` from `assets/sounds/elevenlabs.json`: `cargo run --release -p gen-builtin-sounds` (the output is identical each time). New candidates come from `python3 -I tools/elevenlabs-sounds.py` (ADR-0016).

Every built-in sound was generated by the project author (kaija) with [ElevenLabs Sound Effects](https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects) and is included in Spank with the author's permission. They are **not** covered by CC0 or by the MIT license of the code: to use them outside Spank, ask the author. Originals are in `assets/sounds/source/`; the packs hold mono, -16 LUFS versions.
