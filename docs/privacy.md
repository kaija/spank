# 隱私權政策 · Privacy Policy

適用於 Creaky（Mac App Store 版）與 Spank（DMG 版）。最後更新：2026-10-25。
DMG 版的完整說明（含檢查更新）見 [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md)。

*Applies to Creaky (Mac App Store) and Spank (DMG). Last updated: 2026-10-25.*

## 繁體中文

**我們不收集、不傳送任何資料。** 沒有帳號、沒有分析、沒有廣告、沒有伺服器。

- **所有處理都在你的 Mac 上完成。** 螢幕角度（以及 Spank 的加速度計）讀完就丟，不會保存或送出。
- **設定與你匯入的音效只存在這台 Mac**（Creaky：`~/Library/Containers/dev.spank.app/`；Spank：`~/Library/Application Support/dev.spank.app/`，錯誤紀錄在 `~/Library/Logs/dev.spank.app/`）。
- **從不錄音、錄影。** 為了開會時自動靜音，App 只檢查麥克風或相機「是否正被其他 App 使用」，不擷取任何聲音或影像，也不需要麥克風或相機權限。
- **專注模式**：你允許後，只讀取「專注模式是否開啟」，用來自動靜音。
- **網路**：Creaky 完全不連網路。Spank 只在檢查更新時向 GitHub 下載版本資訊，不送出任何資料。
- **不與任何第三方分享資料**，因為根本沒有資料。

移除 App 並刪除上述資料夾，就不會留下任何東西。問題請到 https://github.com/kaija/spank/issues 。

## English

**We don't collect or transmit any data.** No account, no analytics, no ads, no servers.

- **Everything happens on your Mac.** The lid angle (and, in Spank, the accelerometer) is read and discarded; it is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Settings and the sounds you import stay on this Mac** (Creaky: `~/Library/Containers/dev.spank.app/`; Spank: `~/Library/Application Support/dev.spank.app/`, with error logs in `~/Library/Logs/dev.spank.app/`).
- **Nothing is ever recorded.** To mute itself during calls, the app only checks whether another app is using the microphone or camera. It never captures audio or video and doesn't need microphone or camera permission.
- **Focus**: with your permission, the app reads only whether a Focus is on, to mute itself.
- **Network**: Creaky never connects to the network. Spank only downloads version information from GitHub to check for updates, and sends nothing.
- **No data is shared with third parties**, because there is none.

Delete the app and the folders above, and nothing is left behind. Questions: https://github.com/kaija/spank/issues
